Prototype Name & Description

HAB Business PLC Operations & HRM ERP (Bilingual Web Prototype)

A specialized operational and Human Resource platform designed to manage 24/7 commercial Security Guards and Industrial Cleaning operations across major institutional client facilities in Addis Ababa (Commercial Bank of Ethiopia Tower, Ethiopian Airlines Cargo Hub, Hyatt Regency, St. Paul's Hospital, Bole Medhanialem Complex).

Before Prototyping — Your Learning Plan
Top 3 Learning Questions Tested
  • Q1: Does involving the Store Keeper directly in the digital exit clearance workflow reduce lost uniforms, boots, and gear?
  • Q2: Can field Site Supervisors and Shift Leaders accurately log daily shift attendance using the simplified Amharic interface without continuous IT support?
  • Q3: Does the automated Monday shift rotation eliminate shift clashes and double-booking for hospital and commercial cleaning crews?
Key Metrics for Success (Target Outcomes)
  • Fulfillment Rate: ≥ 98% of contracted guard and cleaner posts staffed on time each shift.
  • Property Return: ≥ 95% uniform and protective gear retrieval rate upon employee separation.
  • Clearance Speed: Exit clearance sign-off time reduced from 10 days to under 48 hours.
  • Usability: ≥ 90% independent roll-call completion rate by non-technical field supervisors in Amharic.
Testing Method & Setup
  • Live Role-Based Testing: Simulated daily operations with 8 distinct user personas (CEO, Legal, Operations Head, HR Head, HR Officers, Store Keeper, Site Supervisor).
  • Simulation Scenarios: Executed end-to-end employee exit clearance, Monday shift rotation, and 24h duty / 48h rest cyclic scheduling.
  • Task Observation: Measured time required to verify Kebele legal guarantors and generate official bilingual service certificates.
Core Assumptions
  • Device Access: Field supervisors have basic mobile smartphone or tablet access at client sites.
  • Physical Custody: Store Keepers inspect physical uniforms and safety boots prior to HR authorizing final severance pay.
  • Legal Standard: 48-hour recovery periods between 24-hour guard shifts comply with Ethiopian Labor Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
