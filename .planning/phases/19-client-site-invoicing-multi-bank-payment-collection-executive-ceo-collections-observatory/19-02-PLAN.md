- **Objective:** Deliver executive-grade accounts receivable telemetry and risk drill-downs for the CEO, featuring an embedded CEO Dashboard collection summary widget, an interactive 4-tier aging analysis visualizer (0–30d, 31–60d, 61–90d, 90+d), a high-risk delinquent accounts alert rail, and a comprehensive slide-over Collections Report Drawer with division breakdowns and CSV export.

## Execution Blueprint

### 1. Collections Telemetry & Aggregation Engine
- Create `src/utils/ceoCollectionsAggregator.ts`:
  - `AgingBucket`:
    - `label`: string (`'Current (0-30d)'` | `'Overdue (31-60d)'` | `'Overdue (61-90d)'` | `'Severely Overdue (90+d)'`).
    - `daysMin`: number; `daysMax`: number;
    - `totalAmountETB`: number;
    - `invoiceCount`: number;
    - `color`: string;
  - `DivisionCollectionSummary`:
    - `division`: `'Security' | 'Cleaning'`;
    - `invoicedETB`: number;
    - `collectedETB`: number;
    - `outstandingETB`: number;
    - `collectionRate`: number;
  - `OverdueRiskSite`:
    - `siteId`: string;
    - `siteName`: string;
    - `clientOrganization`: string;
    - `invoiceNumber`: string;
    - `outstandingBalanceETB`: number;
    - `daysOverdue`: number;
    - `riskLevel`: `'Low' | 'Moderate' | 'High' | 'Critical'`;
  - `CeoCollectionsTelemetry`:
    - `totalInvoicedETB`: number;
    - `totalCollectedETB`: number;
    - `totalOutstandingETB`: number;
    - `collectionRate`: number;
    - `agingBuckets`: `AgingBucket[]`;
    - `divisionSummaries`: `DivisionCollectionSummary[]`;
    - `topOverdueRiskSites`: `OverdueRiskSite[]`;
  - Function `computeCeoCollectionsTelemetry(invoices: ClientInvoice[]): CeoCollectionsTelemetry`.
  - Strict line budget: $\le 150$ lines.

### 2. CEO Collections Summary Widget
- Create `src/components/dashboard/roles/CeoCollectionsSummaryWidget.tsx`:
  - Embedded in `CeoDashboard.tsx` below the executive header.
  - Displays 4 Headline Telemetry Cards:
    - Total Billed Revenue (ETB).
    - Cash Realized / Collected (ETB).
    - Outstanding AR Exposure (ETB).
    - Overall Collection Efficiency Rate (%).
  - 4-Tier Visual Aging Distribution Bar:
    - Proportionate colored segmented bar (Emerald 0–30d, Amber 31–60d, Orange 61–90d, Rose 90+d).
    - Visual legend with tabular monetary figures and percentages.
  - High-Risk Delinquency Alert Strip:
    - Highlights top delinquent clients (e.g., Addis Commercial Center — 60 days overdue, CMC Plaza — 45 days overdue).
    - Status pills indicating risk tier.
    - Quick CTA button: `📊 Launch Collections Observatory` (opens drawer).
  - Strict line budget: $\le 220$ lines.

### 3. CEO Executive Collections Report Drawer
- Create `src/components/dashboard/CeoCollectionsReportDrawer.tsx`:
  - Slide-over executive drawer accessible from CEO Dashboard.
  - Executive Header: Title, Total Portfolio Outstanding, "Print Report" and "Export CSV" buttons.
  - Division Breakdown Strip:
    - Side-by-side cards comparing Security Services vs. Cleaning Services collection velocity and recovery rates.
  - Site Collection Matrix Table:
    - Site Name, Client Org, Division, Invoiced (ETB), Collected (ETB), Withholding Credit (ETB), Outstanding (ETB), Aging Bracket, Payment Status.
    - Filterable by Division and Aging Bracket.
  - Executive Remediation Actions:
    - `📄 Generate Formal Demand Notice`: Generates an official printable legal payment demand letter with client details.
    - `⚠️ Deployment Roster Review`: Flags the site for operational review with Technical Manager to evaluate service continuation.
  - 1-Click CSV export: downloads full collections ledger with date stamp.
  - Strict line budget: $\le 240$ lines.
