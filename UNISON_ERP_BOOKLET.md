# Prototype ÜNISON ERP
**A Human-Centered ERP Solution for HAB Business PLC**  
*Different Services. One Purpose.*

---

## Problem

* **The problem**  
  The main problem is that HAB Business PLC employees and management, operating across security services and cleaning services, currently manage critical business activities through separate systems, paper logbooks, and manual spreadsheets. This creates fragmented information, duplicate data entry, reporting delays, coordination difficulties, and challenges in making timely, informed operational decisions.

* **Our target group**  
  Our target group is frontline security guards, custodial cleaning personnel, and operations cluster supervisors deployed across over 100 commercial client facilities in Addis Ababa. These field workers operate in round-the-clock rotations and need simple, transparent digital tools for daily post check-in, shift handovers, and consumable replenishment without administrative friction.

* **Our main insight and learnings from the field trip**  
  Our main insight from the field trip was that frontline staff and supervisors are burdened by redundant manual data entry, spending up to four hours traveling between client sites just to cross-check paper attendance logbooks. Frontline teams do not want complex, burdensome software—they need an ultra-simple, dependable interface that guarantees accurate shift attendance, immediate relief coordination, and transparent service records.

---

## Solution / Prototype

* **Our Prototype**  
  Our prototype, **ÜNISON ERP**, is a human-centered enterprise platform designed to unify HAB Business PLC’s two core divisions—**Security Services** and **Cleaning Services**—into a single digital ecosystem. By applying human-centered design principles, the platform digitizes post-by-post guard rostering, custodial cleaning inspection checklists, shift relief scheduling, and 3D warehouse inventory tracking for uniforms and cleaning chemicals. It replaces isolated paper records with synchronized real-time workflows connecting frontline field staff, supervisors, and executive management.

## Our biggest learning as a team

Our biggest learning as a team was that an ERP system for service operations is fundamentally about people, not just data. Initially, we viewed the problem as a database synchronization challenge. However, through human-centered design research with guards, cleaners, and field supervisors, we discovered that the true bottleneck was cognitive fatigue and fragmented communication. We learned that software built for frontline teams must be ultra-simple, resilient to field distractions, and designed with empathy. By unifying two distinct service departments into one harmonious interface—**ÜNISON**—we experienced how human-centered design can transform disconnected daily labor into a cohesive, dignified, and synchronized workforce.

## Description

> **ÜNISON ERP** is an integrated digital workforce deployment and operations platform for the specific purpose of supporting the digitization of field shift rostering, custodial cleaning quality checklists, and uniform/consumable warehouse inventory for frontline guards, cleaners, and operational supervisors across over 100 commercial facilities in Addis Ababa.
